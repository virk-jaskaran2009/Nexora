Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

Nexora AI Forge is an AI automation and digital solutions agency based in Delhi NCR, India. This policy explains what happens to the information you give us through this website. You can reach us at nexora.aiforge@gmail.com or through the WhatsApp link on our contact page.

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form, we receive only what you type into it:

  • Your name, email address and phone / WhatsApp number
  • Company or business name (only if you choose to give it)
  • The service you're interested in and your budget range (if provided)
  • Your message, and how you'd prefer to be contacted

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or session recordings. This website does not set tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote or consultation
  • To continue the conversation if you decide to work with us
  • To keep a simple record of business enquiries (so nothing gets lost)

We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone for marketing.

How it is sent and stored

Form submissions go to a secure server-side endpoint on our website host, which filters spam and forwards the enquiry to our business inbox. Enquiries may also be stored in our own contact database so we can track the conversation. Only people working on your project can see it.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors to run this site:

  • Netlify — website hosting and form delivery
  • Resend — email delivery for enquiry notifications
  • Supabase — our enquiry database (if configured)
  • Google Fonts — font files used by the design

They process data only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept only for as long as they are useful for responding to you and managing the conversation. Ask us at any time and we'll delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it — just email us with the address above. We'll respond under applicable Indian data protection law.

Children

This website is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If the way we handle enquiries changes, we'll update this page and revise the date shown at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email nexora.aiforge@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp. We're based in Delhi NCR, India.
