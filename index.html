How it works

From first call to live system in four steps

No endless discovery phase. You'll know the price, the timeline and what you're getting before we write a line of code.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    Thirty minutes. We walk through your processes and pinpoint the tasks worth automating first — usually the ones everyone dreads.

  2. 02

    Blueprint & fixed quote

    You get a written scope, a timeline and one price. No hourly billing, no surprise line items at the end of the month.

  3. 03

    Build & integrate

    We build in weekly check-ins, connect the tools you already use, and test everything on your real data before it goes anywhere near a customer.

  4. 04

    Launch & support

    We go live, train your team and keep an eye on the system. Fixes and tweaks are included for the first 30 days.
